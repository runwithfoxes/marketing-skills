# Market research

Finds out what a market is actually saying, from sources, and where the gap is. It always applies the ten rules in `research-rules.md`. A worked set of research files for a made-up insurance market is in `examples/kite/research/`.

## How to work

1. Ask what decision the research is for, and write the questions down before opening a single source.
2. Read `research-rules.md` and follow it.
3. Report what the sources say, each finding with its source, kept apart from what you think it means.
