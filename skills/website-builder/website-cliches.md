# Website cliches

These are seventeen looks that are common right now. Most of them came up again and again in 18 AI-built website templates we looked at in October 2026. None of them is good or bad. They're easy to do, so they become commonplace, and everything can start to look the same. Tom Roach at BBH calls it the sea of sameness (https://www.bbh-labs.com/the-stupidity-of-sameness-and-the-value-of-difference), and Alex Murrell calls it the age of average (https://www.alexmurrell.co.uk/articles/the-age-of-average). Both are worth reading.

Give this file to Claude before it builds anything, and ask it to tell you whenever it reaches for one of these. When it does, ask what would make that part of the page yours instead. Come back to the list every few months, because what's common keeps changing, and once your site lives in Claude Code, changing it is quick.

1. **Tiny spaced capitals above the headline.** A small label in letters spread wide. It's one line of styling, so a lot of templates start with it.
2. **The two-part headline.** Two short phrases and two full stops, like Less noise. More growth. It's an easy shape to fill in, so a lot of pages now say things the same way.
3. **The italic word.** One word in the headline set in an italic serif. It's a quick way to add feeling, and it's on the rise right now.
4. **A brand that could be anyone's.** A warm off-white page, a soft serif and one orange button. It came up again and again on the AI-built templates we looked at.
5. **Two buttons with an arrow.** A filled button with an arrow and a plain one beside it. Templates come with the pair, so a lot of pages have it.
6. **The floating dashboard.** A picture of the product in a rounded window, tilted a little, with a soft shadow. One screenshot and the template does the rest.
7. **The grey logo row.** Trusted by, then a row of logos washed out to grey, in the same place on a lot of launch pages.
8. **Matching cards with small icons.** Three cards the same size, each with an icon in a rounded square, a title and two lines of grey text. A lot of templates have this block ready to fill.
9. **The scrolling band of words.** Single words in capitals sliding past, with dots between them. It's easy to add, and it moves.
10. **The centred quote.** One line in italics with a small round photo, a first name and CEO at somewhere.
11. **The exact big number.** Join 12,847+ marketers. The odd number is meant to make it feel real. Once a lot of pages do it, it stops feeling real.
12. **Three steps.** One, two, three, with a line each. It's a ready-made block in a lot of templates.
13. **The pricing switch.** Monthly or yearly, save 20%, and the middle plan marked Most popular.
14. **Questions? Answered.** A question, a full stop and an answer as the title, over a list that opens one line at a time.
15. **The giant faded word.** A huge word in pale outline behind a section, like WORK or SERVICES.
16. **The dark band with a glow.** A black section with a neon light behind a line. It looks like the future, which is why a lot of templates now look like the same future.
17. **One long page.** Everything stacked on one page, top to bottom: headline, logos, cards, quote, prices, questions. It's the shape a lot of templates start you with, so it's easy to end up the same shape as everyone else.
