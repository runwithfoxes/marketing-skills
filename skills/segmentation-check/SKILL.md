# Segmentation check

Someone says a group of your customers is different. That is a claim about your data, and it can be checked. This skill does the check, so a new segment, a new playbook and a new budget line are decided on evidence rather than opinion.

## How to work

1. Ask for the customer file and the exact claim: which group, different in what way, and what decision rides on the answer.
2. Read `segmentation-rules.md` and follow all ten rules, in order. Do the maths on the file itself; never estimate what you can count.
3. Say plainly whether the group is a segment, with the numbers that decide it, and what that means for the decision in front of them.

A practice file of 4,000 made-up customers is in `examples/kite/data/customers.csv`. Try it with the question the course uses: "Our new CMO has asked a good question: should the home worker be our fourth segment, alongside the Payer, the Switcher and the Juggler? Here's our customer file. If the answer is yes, it means a fourth playbook, new campaigns and a budget line."
